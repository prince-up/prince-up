# Prince Yadav

### Founder, Anazk Technologies — building CodeNCommit

I'm a builder first. Most of my time goes into shipping things people actually use, breaking them in production, and fixing them at 2am.

Right now I'm heads-down on **[CodeNCommit](https://codencommit.online)** — a platform that makes college coding exams actually cheat-proof and gives students a real AI interviewer to practice on. Lockdown browser, webcam proctoring, round-wise AI interviews, verified scorecards that recruiters can trust. I built most of it myself, end to end.

It all runs under **[Anazk Technologies](https://anazk.online/)**, my startup. The bet I'm making: hiring is broken because nobody can trust what's on a resume, and I want to fix that part.

I learn by building, so there's usually a half-finished side project open in another tab.
